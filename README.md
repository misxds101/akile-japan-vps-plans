## JPHyper vs JPBase vs JPIIJ vs JPPro: what the four lines actually differ in

The four names are separate product lines, and the published differences are port speed, traffic allowance, disk size and price per unit of traffic. That last one is the useful number, because it lets you compare plans that look nothing alike.

Converted from the annual prices above (annual price ÷ annual traffic, so this is arithmetic on Akile's list, not an official rate card):

| JPIIJ | Starter, Standard, Pro | 1000M | ≈¥18.6–24.3 |
| JPPro | Max, Ultra | 1200M | ≈¥67–73 |

The pattern: JPHyper traffic is the cheapest on the Japan list, and its price scales almost linearly with traffic. Mini, Starter and Standard all land within a few percent of each other per TB, which tells you CPU and RAM are not what drives the price. Doubling traffic roughly doubles the bill.

IIJ traffic costs about 2.3× what JPHyper traffic costs at the same volume. Whether that premium buys you anything depends on routing, and here the published data runs out: there are no latency figures, no traceroutes and no mainland-China connectivity tests for any of these lines. Anyone ranking JPHyper above JPIIJ, or the reverse, is guessing. What you can say is that IIJ is a named upstream and JPBase's unlimited plans, at 500M and 1000M shared, are the slowest ports on the list.

## Limits worth knowing before you pay

**Streaming unlock is DNS-level, not a platform guarantee.** Akile configures DNS streaming unlock on its nodes. That is not the same as promising a specific service works from a specific IP, and IP ranges get reassessed by streaming platforms regularly.

**Mainland China direct connection is not guaranteed.** This is stated plainly on Akile's own product documentation for its node families, and it should override any looser marketing phrasing you see elsewhere. If a low-latency mainland link is the whole reason you're shopping, test before you commit to a year.

**Over-traffic handling is documented for some lines, not for Japan.** For the Hong Kong NTT, Singapore GSL and Los Angeles LAX Lite series, Akile states that traffic beyond the plan limit is throttled to 10Mbps shared, with extra bandwidth available for purchase. Confirm the equivalent rule for the specific Japan node on the order page before you rely on it.

**Most JPHyper plans ship with a 10GB disk.** After the OS there is very little left. If you're storing media or running containers, the 20GB plans (JPHyper-Standard and up) matter more than the CPU count.

**Everything here is annual billing.** There is no verified monthly price for any Japan plan in this list. Budget the full year.
